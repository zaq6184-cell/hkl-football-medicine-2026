THE MEDICAL SET PIECE

A pitchside emergency should be rehearsed before kickoff: clear plan, clear roles, correct positioning, ready equipment and closed-loop communication.

  • Team doctor enters first, followed by the pitchside medical team.
  • Match doctor – highest rank (Black).

BEFORE KICKOFF: 4 KEY PEAP COMPONENTS

M — Meeting

Who is involved?

  • Home team doctor
  • Visiting team doctor
  • Stadium medical officer
  • Pitchside paramedics

C — Contacts

Who do we call and where do we go?

  • Emergency numbers
  • Access / egress gates
  • Ambulance location
  • Receiving hospital

R — Roles & positioning

Who does what and where?

  • Assign TL + M1–M5
  • Assign extrication crew
  • Confirm positions
  • Brief / rehearse

E — Equipment

Is everything ready?

  • Emergency care bag
  • AED + pads
  • Oxygen + BVM
  • Scoop / spinal board / basket + straps

M-CRE = Meet → Call → Position → Check

GOLDEN RULE: NON-CONTACT COLLAPSE

Presume sudden cardiac arrest (SCA) until proven otherwise

ENTER IMMEDIATELY → ASSESS → START CPR → APPLY AED WITHIN 30 SECONDS. Do not delay CPR / defibrillation for C-spine or fracture assessment in a non-contact collapse.

PITCHSIDE RESUSCITATION TEAM

RolePositionMain responsibility
Team Leader (Black)Left flank, 2–3 m back; full viewHands off. Overall command, CPR/AED flow, calls the lift for extrication, ambulance/referee liaison.
M1 Head (Orange)At headMILS, airway alignment; commands the log-roll / tilt count.
M2 Chest (Red)Right chestABCDE, continuous CPR 100–120/min, full recoil.
M3 Oxygen (Blue)Right hipO2 15 L/min; takes PELVIS in log-roll; oxygen OFF for shocks.
M4 Legs / Extrication (White)Right legExtrication kit (trauma: scoop + basket; arrest: spinal board ± basket), splints; takes LEGS in log-roll.
M5 Equipment (Green)Left head/shoulderAED, emergency bag, equipment to M2; CLEAR + oxygen off before shock; relieves compressions at 2 min.

FIFA ECM: the Chest member should be one of the most experienced — they lead if no one else can. Pelvis and Legs need no medical skills but must be trained in the log roll. First aiders and paramedics only help if they are part of the plan.

PITCHSIDE POSITIONING & TEAM ERGONOMICS

Equipment zone
AED + O2 + emergency care bag at the player's head / left side.
Extrication zone
Extrication kit (scoop or spinal board, basket) aligned at the feet, initially kept back until required.
Team Leader
Remain 2–3 m away with a full view. Keep the working area clean and uncluttered.

EXTRICATION SET PIECE

PREPARE → TILT → SCOOP → STRAP → BASKET → GO

  • M1 commands a gentle 15–20 degree tilt.
  • Reconnect the scoop head first, then feet.
  • Strap: Chest → Pelvis → Legs → Head.
Medical kit (FIFA)
FIFA bag + oxygen to one side of the head; one person hands out equipment.
Extrication kit (FIFA)
Basket in line with the feet, 2–3 basket lengths away. Scoop out of the basket, extended and split between basket and player.
Extrication team (yellow)
Stand or kneel beside the scoop / basket facing the player; wait for the team leader. One stays free to pass the scoop and slide the basket.

Trauma: scoop stretcher + basket. Cardiac arrest: spinal board ± basket. The scoop / basket set piece above is for trauma; for an arrest in progress, log-roll onto the spinal board (± basket for the carry) — see Station 4.

CLOSED-LOOP COMMUNICATION

NAME → COMMAND → REPEAT → CONFIRM

Example — TL: “M5, apply AED.” M5: “Applying AED.” M5: “AED attached.”
