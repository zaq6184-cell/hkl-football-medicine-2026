STATION 4

Approach to Pitch-side Cardiac Arrest

Part 1 — BLS + AED · Part 2 — 10-second stop and start for ambulance transfer

STATION EQUIPMENT (10)
  • CPR manikin5
  • Airway manikin1
  • AED trainer5
  • BVM5

  • OPA1
  • Oxygen tank (dummy)1

WORKSHOP STANDARD

Follows the HKL SCA Colour Role Cards

First shock before the board. Then Orange (the head) controls the log roll; White + Yellow First Aiders slide in the spinal board; player + spinal board + AED move as one strapped unit (± basket for the carry). Green takes over compressions at 2 minutes. Carry ≤ 10 seconds on Black's “LIFT”, then board down and at least 2 minutes of compressions.

TEAM MEMBER ROLES & EQUIPMENT

Team Leader — brings: Radio / comms. PEAP contact card.

BLACK
  • Player's LEFT flank, mid-torso (FIFA PEAP black mark). Hands-off. Not at the feet.

Airway / C-spine — brings: LMA (i-gel) and BVM with reservoir.

ORANGE
  • At the player's HEAD (FIFA PEAP orange mark). Never leave the head.

Chest — brings: Nothing. First hands on the player.

RED
  • Player's RIGHT CHEST (FIFA PEAP red mark).

Oxygen — brings: Oxygen cylinder.

BLUE
  • Player's RIGHT HIP (FIFA PEAP blue mark), same side as Red.

Spinal board ± basket — brings: Spinal board, spider straps and splints (± basket stretcher for the carry).

WHITE
  • Player's RIGHT LEG, level with the lower legs, same side as Red and Blue (FIFA PEAP white mark).

Bag + AED — brings: FIFA emergency care bag and AED.

GREEN
  • At the HEAD with the bag and AED (FIFA PEAP green mark).

First Aiders — brings: Nothing required. Spinal board (± basket), splints and spider straps if directed.

YELLOW
  • At the FEET, in an arc facing the player (FIFA PEAP yellow marks). Not medical staff by default.

STEP BY STEP

1 · Assess (≤ 10 s check)

REDBLACK
  • Non-contact collapse = SCA. Enter at once — do not wait for the ball to go out of play.
  • Red: priority is signs of life. “Player is unresponsive!” … “No breathing, no pulse. No signs of life!”
  • Gasping or slow seizure-like movement = SCA, not a fit.
  • Red starts CONTINUOUS compressions (5–6 cm, 100–120/min, full recoil) and calls for the AED.
  • Black ensures the ambulance is called: “Sudden cardiac arrest protocol activated. Ambulance on standby for immediate field entry.”

2 · Airway + O2 + AED (At once)

ORANGEBLUEGREENRED
  • Green: AED on now — do not wait for the current 30-count. Pads upper-right chest + lower-left axilla.
  • Red does not stop compressions while the pads go on.
  • Orange — once compressions + AED are running: jaw thrust, i-gel / LMA, bag once every 10 compressions. Face mask only or i-gel leaking: 30:2.
  • Blue: oxygen 15 L/min to the bag / reservoir.

3 · Analyse + first shock (Hands off)

REDORANGEBLUEGREEN
  • AED: “Stop CPR. Analysing.” Red hovers hands above the chest; Orange pauses ventilations.
  • Shock advised: Blue turns oxygen OFF (do not disconnect the i-gel). Green checks all clear.
  • Green: “I'm clear, you're clear, everyone's clear!” Shock. “Shock delivered.”
  • Red resumes compressions at once — no pulse check.

4 · Spinal board (Orange's count)

BLACKORANGEREDBLUEWHITEYELLOW
  • Black: “Orange, you have the roll.”
  • Orange: “Roll on three. One, two, three, ROLL.” Red chest · Blue pelvis · White legs.
  • White + Yellow First Aiders slide the spinal board underneath (board was 2–3 lengths from the feet).
  • Strap: player + spinal board + AED = one unit (± basket for the carry). White: “Board is in.”
  • CPR continues on the board until the next AED analysis (2 min). Green takes over compressions at 2 min.

5 · Shock + 10-second dash (≤ 10 s)

GREENBLUEBLACKYELLOW
  • Next analysis: shock if advised (Blue oxygen OFF, Green clear).
  • The moment the shock finishes, Black: “Ready to lift. On three. One, two, three, LIFT.”
  • Yellow First Aiders: “Lifting on your command, Black.” Carry ≤ 10 s — keep pace, stay silent.
  • Green times the window out loud: “Ten seconds!” Board down. CPR for at least 2 minutes.

6 · Continue (Until ambulance)

BLACKGREEN
  • Repeat analysis → shock if advised → 10-second dash → board down → CPR ≥ 2 min until the player is inside the ambulance.
  • Prefer the ambulance onto the field.
  • ALS if trained: adrenaline 1 mg IV after the 3rd shock, every 3–5 min; amiodarone 300 mg after the 3rd shock.

AIRWAY & VENTILATION IN ARREST

  • i-gel is FIFA's adjunct of choice: one person holds it and squeezes the bag.
  • Bag with reservoir on oxygen at 15 L/min — better than a face mask + Guedel (two-person, poorer seal).
  • Continuous compressions, one breath every 10th compression. Leak → pause for 30:2.
  • Let the bag refill after each squeeze. Watch for symmetrical chest rise.
  • No i-gel: use the adjunct you know best (OPA or NPA) with a face mask.

ADVANCED LIFE SUPPORT (IF TRAINED)

RhythmDrug
Shockable (VF / pVT)Adrenaline 1 mg IV after the 3rd shock, then every 3–5 min. Amiodarone 300 mg after the 3rd shock; 150 mg after the 5th.
Non-shockable (asystole / PEA)Adrenaline 1 mg IV as soon as possible, then every 3–5 min.

Good compressions and early defibrillation matter most — do not let ALS distract from them. Reversible causes on the pitch: hypoxia, hypovolaemia, hypo/hyperthermia, tension pneumothorax.
